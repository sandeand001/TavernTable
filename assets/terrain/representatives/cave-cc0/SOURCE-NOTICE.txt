Selected CC0 cave-model derivatives

mine-frame.glb and stalagmite-cluster.glb:
Free Mine Assets by rubberduck, CC0 1.0 Universal
https://opengameart.org/content/free-mine-assets-pack
Source objects: Wooden_14 and stalags_1_high in mine_parts.blend.
Exported to GLB with replacement materials; the stalagmite uses flat shading
and an embedded stone texture. The entrance frame is decorative and does not
create terrain.

stone-column.glb:
Low Poly Cave Assets by agdg reject, CC0 1.0 Universal
https://opengameart.org/content/low-poly-cave-assets
Source object: cave_pillar1.obj. Exported to GLB with a replacement textured
material and flat shading; the source MTL was not supplied.

The embedded stone texture is derived from the CC0 rock1.png included in Free
Mine Assets, credited by the pack creator to Yughues. It was resized to 512px,
converted to grayscale and tone-adjusted for the application's cave lighting.

CC0 1.0 Universal: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Source and derivative fingerprints are recorded in ASSET_MANIFEST.json.
