Downloaded source packs
Locally retained source packs are recorded under the terrain and animated-sprite
source-pack manifests. Their archives and extracted contents are excluded from
the frontend package; selected representative copies listed below are active
runtime assets. Several creators permit commercial incorporation but prohibit
redistribution of the raw asset files. The source packs remain local-only and
git-ignored. See SOURCE-PACK-MANIFEST.json and each pack's SOURCE-NOTICE.txt.
